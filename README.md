# ArchimedesLS
Graphical User Interfacte to compute the density of liquids and solids using the Archimedes principles. The GUI analyze the uncertainty of the liquid and solid density. The GUI was written with Tkinter.

For the calculation of the liquid density, measure the masses of three solid standards with known densities in air and immerged in the liquid. Multiple measurements are recommended to define the standard deviation of the mass. The slope is the density of the liquid. For the calculation of the density of a solid sample, the density of the liquid needs to be known and the masses of the solids needs to be measured in air and immerged in the liquid.
